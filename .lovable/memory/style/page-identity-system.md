# Page Identity System

Every page must feel like its own experience while clearly belonging to one brand. No two pages should share the same emotional rhythm or visual cadence.

## Universal Brand Constants (apply to ALL pages)
- Typography: Cormorant Garamond headings, DM Sans body
- Color system: Forest green, charcoal, warm white, highland gold accents
- Gold accent ≤8% surface area
- Premium language — no gimmicky CTAs
- Mobile-first responsive
- Tartan/blueprint textures used structurally, never decoratively

---

## 1. HOMEPAGE (`/`)

**Strategic Purpose:** First impression powerhouse. Prove Highlander is the standard — not just another contractor. Convert explorers into believers within 10 seconds.

**Emotional Tone:** Cinematic confidence. Quiet power. "We don't need to convince you — just show you."

**Visual Mood:** Dark-to-light editorial flow. Cinematic hero with video parallax. High contrast. Atmospheric depth. Golden-hour warmth.

**Section Order:**
1. Hero (cinematic video, three-line reveal)
2. TrustStrip (credential proof)
3. DualPathway (Roofing vs Construction — equal weight)
4. ValueProposition + SilentObjections
5. ProofMoment (credentials)
6. ServicesGrid (both divisions)
7. ProjectPathfinder (interactive)
8. ConstructionAuthority (discipline bridge)
9. BuiltForWNC (local authority)
10. OurProcess
11. FeaturedProjects (visual proof)
12. MeetTheTeam
13. BlogInsights + Trust + TownGrid
14. ProjectConcierge (premium CTA)
15. InspectionForm + CTABlock

**Content Emphasis:** Brand positioning, dual-division authority, local expertise, social proof volume

**Proof Style:** Stats-heavy (500+, 4.9★, 150+ reviews). Credential badges. Volume signals.

**CTA Style:** "Start Your Project" (primary gold gradient). Dual-path in closing (Roofing / Construction).

**Motion Style:** Dramatic — long cinematic reveals (1.1s+), parallax hero, gold line animations, staggered entrance sequences. Most theatrical page on the site.

## 2. ROOFING DIVISION (`/roofing`)

**Strategic Purpose:** Establish Highlander as the definitive roofing authority in WNC. Channel visitors to specific service pages.

**Emotional Tone:** Technical authority meets mountain wisdom. "We've seen every roof fail at elevation — and we know why."

**Visual Mood:** Green-accented. Tartan textures. Weather imagery. Performance-driven aesthetic — structural, protective, engineered.

**Section Order:**
1. Division Hero (green accent, breadcrumb)
2. Opening Statement (centered, philosophical)
3. Service Categories (card grid linking to subpages)
4. Material Guidance (elevation-specific)
5. MidCTA (roofing consultation)
6. Trust Framework (certifications, warranty)
7. Process (roofing-specific steps)
8. Gallery (roofing projects)
9. FAQs (roofing category)
10. ClosingCTA

**Content Emphasis:** Technical roofing knowledge, material science at elevation, certification authority, weather resilience

**Proof Style:** Certification-led (CertainTeed Master, GAF Master Elite). "Top 1% nationally." Warranty documentation emphasis.

**CTA Style:** "Request a Roof Consultation" — green-toned, performance-language

**Motion Style:** Precise and controlled. Clean reveals (0.5-0.7s). Curtain-reveal on images. Measured, engineered feeling — like the materials themselves.

## 3. CONSTRUCTION DIVISION (`/construction`)

**Strategic Purpose:** Prove Construction is a real, substantial division — not a side hustle. Position Highlander as design-build capable.

**Emotional Tone:** Architectural aspiration. "We build spaces that change how you live." Warmer, more lifestyle-oriented than Roofing.

**Visual Mood:** Gold-accented. Blueprint textures. Warmer section backgrounds. Architectural line details (arch-corners). Spatial, lifestyle imagery.

**Section Order:**
1. Division Hero (gold accent, breadcrumb)
2. Opening Statement
3. ServiceGrid (5 construction categories, detailed)
4. WNC Relevance (local construction factors)
5. MidCTA (construction consultation)
6. Trust (dark, with objection display)
7. Process (compact 6-step)
8. Gallery (construction projects)
9. FAQs (division category)
10. ClosingCTA

**Content Emphasis:** Project scope, planning discipline, design-build capability, lifestyle transformation, WNC terrain challenges

**Proof Style:** Process-led. "Same documented process, same in-house crews, same warranty." Discipline-bridge from roofing credibility.

**CTA Style:** "Schedule a Construction Consultation" — gold-toned, project-language. Routes to `/construction/consultation`.

**Motion Style:** Measured and spatial. Blueprint grid as ambient texture. Gold corner reveals on cards. Slightly slower transitions (0.6-0.8s) — architectural patience. Less theatrical than homepage, more deliberate.

## 4. PROJECTS / GALLERY (`/gallery`)

**Strategic Purpose:** Visual proof engine. Let the work speak. Convert skeptics through undeniable quality evidence.

**Emotional Tone:** "See for yourself." Gallery confidence. Minimal text, maximum visual impact. Let craftsmanship do the talking.

**Visual Mood:** Clean, gallery-white backgrounds. Minimal UI chrome. Images as heroes. Dark lightbox overlay for detail viewing. Photography-forward.

**Section Order:**
1. Gallery Hero (minimal — title + filter controls)
2. Filter Bar (division, type, material)
3. Project Grid (masonry or uniform cards)
4. Lightbox (premium, full-bleed)
5. Soft CTA (after browsing — "Inspired? Let's discuss your project.")

**Content Emphasis:** Project photography, before/after, material choices, location context (mountain settings visible)

**Proof Style:** Visual-only. No stats. No badges. The images ARE the proof. Captions show location + scope.

**CTA Style:** Soft, non-interruptive. "Ready to discuss your project?" appears after scroll depth or lightbox close. Never aggressive in a gallery.

**Motion Style:** Smooth and subtle. Image scale on hover (1.03x). Fade-in grid items. Lightbox slide transitions. The quietest page motion-wise — images need stillness to land.

## 5. ABOUT (`/about`)

**Strategic Purpose:** Build personal trust. Make the company feel human, local, and values-driven. Answer "who are these people?"

**Emotional Tone:** Warm pride. "We're your neighbors who happen to be really good at this." Human, grounded, genuine.

**Visual Mood:** Warm tones. Team photography prominent. More cream/warm backgrounds than other pages. Personal, editorial quality — like a magazine profile piece.

**Section Order:**
1. About Hero (team-focused, warmer tone)
2. Origin Story (narrative, editorial)
3. Brand Values (visual cards)
4. Team Section (photos, roles, personalities)
5. Why WNC (local commitment, not just business)
6. Certifications & Credentials
7. Community Involvement
8. CTA (conversational — "Want to meet us? Start a conversation.")

**Content Emphasis:** People, values, local roots, company story, why they care about quality

**Proof Style:** Story-driven. Not stats — narratives. "How we started." "Why we're selective." Team bios with personality.

**CTA Style:** Conversational. "Talk With Our Team" — the warmest, most personal CTA on the site. No urgency.

**Motion Style:** Gentle and warm. Slow fades (0.8-1s). No dramatic reveals. Subtle parallax on team photos. Feels like turning pages in a book — unhurried, personal.

## 6. BLOG (`/blog`)

**Strategic Purpose:** SEO authority engine + education hub. Position Highlander as the most knowledgeable contractor in WNC. Drive organic traffic.

**Emotional Tone:** Expert educator. "Here's what we know — and what you should know too." Generous with knowledge, never gatekeeping.

**Visual Mood:** Clean editorial. Magazine-like layouts. Generous whitespace. Featured images prominent. Category color coding (green for roofing, gold for construction).

**Section Order:**
1. Blog Hero (featured post spotlight)
2. Category Filter (Roofing / Construction / Storm / Guides / WNC)
3. Post Grid (cards with category badges, read time, date)
4. Sidebar: Popular posts, consultation CTA, storm alert
5. Individual posts: long-form, image-rich, internal links

**Content Emphasis:** Educational depth, local relevance, seasonal topics, material guides, storm preparedness

**Proof Style:** Knowledge-as-proof. Detailed technical articles demonstrate expertise better than badges. Internal links to relevant service pages.

**CTA Style:** Contextual. Blog sidebar: "Need Expert Advice?" In-article: soft inline CTAs related to post topic. Never interrupts reading flow.

**Motion Style:** Minimal and fast. Card hover lifts. Quick fade-ins (0.3-0.4s). Reading experience should feel fast and clean — no animation competing with content.

## 7. CONTACT (`/contact`)

**Strategic Purpose:** Remove all friction between intent and action. Make reaching Highlander feel easy, personal, and premium.

**Emotional Tone:** Welcoming efficiency. "We're right here. Let's talk." Direct, warm, zero-friction.

**Visual Mood:** Split layout — form on one side, contact info + map on the other. Clean, functional, premium but not flashy. Trust signals prominent near form.

**Section Order:**
1. Contact Hero (brief — phone number immediately visible)
2. Dual Panel: Form | Contact Info + Map
3. Office Hours + Response Time Guarantee
4. Trust Strip (credentials near form)
5. FAQ (contact-specific: "What happens after I reach out?")

**Content Emphasis:** Multiple contact methods (phone, form, email). Response time promise. What to expect after contact. Office location context.

**Proof Style:** Trust-proximity. Credentials placed physically near the form to reduce submission anxiety. "24-hour response" promise.

**CTA Style:** Form IS the CTA. Submit button: "Send Your Message." Below: "Or call (828) 397-9211 — we answer our own phone."

**Motion Style:** Almost none. Form fields with subtle focus animations. Trust badges fade in. The most utilitarian page — motion would slow down conversion.

## 8. SERVICE AREAS (`/service-areas`)

**Strategic Purpose:** Local SEO powerhouse. Prove deep WNC knowledge for every town served. Drive town-specific organic traffic.

**Emotional Tone:** Local pride. "We don't just serve these towns — we build in them every week."

**Visual Mood:** Map-centric. Town cards with local photography. Green and gold division indicators per town.

**Section Order:**
1. Service Areas Hero
2. Interactive Town Grid (searchable)
3. Town Cards (primary vs secondary areas)
4. Division Availability per Area
5. CTA (location-aware)

**Content Emphasis:** Town names, counties, driving distances, local project examples, terrain/weather specifics per area

**Proof Style:** Geographic specificity. "12 projects completed in Highlands." Local knowledge signals per town.

**CTA Style:** Location-contextual. "Discuss your [Town Name] project."

**Motion Style:** Light. Card hover effects. Map interactions. Quick, functional animations.

---

## 9. REVIEWS (`/reviews`)

**Strategic Purpose:** Social proof aggregation. Overwhelming volume of positive feedback in one place.

**Emotional Tone:** Earned confidence. "Don't take our word for it." Let customers speak.

**Visual Mood:** Clean testimonial layouts. Star ratings prominent. Customer names and towns visible. Mix of card and full-width quote formats.

**Proof Style:** Volume + specificity. Named reviewers, specific project types, town names. Real words, not curated marketing quotes.

**CTA Style:** After scroll depth: "Ready to become our next success story?"

**Motion Style:** Staggered card reveals. Quote fade-ins. Moderate — proof needs to feel authentic, not produced.
