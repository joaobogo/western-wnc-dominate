# Construction Division Messaging Framework

## Core Positioning Statement
Highlander's Construction division is the natural extension of a company that already proved it could plan meticulously, execute precisely, and communicate proactively — on 500+ roofs. Now the same discipline applies to every addition, renovation, and custom build we accept.

## Voice Rules
- **Tone:** Calm authority. Selective confidence. Never salesy, never desperate.
- **POV:** Always "we" — never "I", never generic third person.
- **Selectivity signal:** We don't take every project. We choose work where our standards deliver the most value.
- **Proof before promise:** Lead with systems and track record, not adjectives.
- **WNC grounding:** Every claim connects to mountain terrain, weather, or architecture.

## CTA Language (Construction Context)
- **Primary:** "Schedule a Project Consultation"
- **Secondary:** "Discuss Your Project"
- **Mid-page:** "Talk With Our Construction Team"
- **Closing:** "Schedule a Project Consultation"
- **Phone:** "Call Direct" (never "Call Now")
- **Hub exploration:** "Explore the Construction Division"
- **Subpage links:** "Learn More" (never "Get Started")

## Forbidden Language
Never use:
- "Full-service contractor" / "We do it all" / "One-stop shop"
- "Free estimate" / "Free inspection" / "No obligation"
- "Affordable" / "Budget-friendly" / "Competitive pricing"
- "Book now" / "Act now" / "Limited availability"
- "Dream home" / "Transform your space" / "Make your vision a reality"
- "Top-rated" / "Award-winning" (unless citing specific award)
- Any urgency or scarcity language

## Proof Points (Always Available)
- Licensed General Contractor
- In-House Crews (not subcontracted)
- 8 WNC Counties served
- Design-Build Capable
- Full Liability & Workers' Comp
- CertainTeed Master Applicator (roofing credential as proof of standards)

## Objection-Response Tone
Every trust claim maps to a specific homeowner fear and addresses it with **systems and documentation** — never vague reassurance.
- Wrong: "Don't worry, we're the best."
- Right: "You receive a written scope with defined deliverables, specified materials, a confirmed timeline, and line-item pricing before any commitment."
