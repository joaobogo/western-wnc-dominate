# Brand Values Framework

## The Seven Values

### 1. Craftsmanship
- **Definition:** Every cut, seam, and fastener is executed to a standard that outlasts the weather it was built for.
- **Trust Card:** "No Shortcuts at 4,000 Feet" — Manufacturer-exact installation. Owner-inspected at every phase.
- **Team Statement:** "We don't build to pass inspection. We build to last decades."
- **CTA Support:** "Work with a team that treats your roof like they'll drive past it every day."
- **Visual:** Detail closeup of perfect flashing work or ridge cap installation

### 2. Integrity
- **Definition:** Honest assessments. No upselling. Written scope before work begins. No surprise change orders.
- **Trust Card:** "What We Say Is What We Do" — Written scope, fixed pricing, photo documentation.
- **Team Statement:** "We'd rather lose a job than sell you something you don't need."
- **CTA Support:** "Get a straight answer about your roof — not a sales pitch."
- **Visual:** Written scope document on clipboard, clean estimate format

### 3. Communication
- **Definition:** Named point of contact. Daily updates. You'll never wonder what's happening on your project.
- **Trust Card:** "You'll Always Know What's Happening" — 24hr response, daily updates, named contact.
- **Team Statement:** "We answer our phones. We return calls the same day. We explain things in plain language."
- **CTA Support:** "From your first call to your final walkthrough, you'll always know exactly where things stand."
- **Visual:** Team member on phone at jobsite, daily update text screenshot

### 4. Long-Term Value
- **Definition:** We build for the next 30 years, not just the next inspection. Every material and method is chosen for longevity.
- **Trust Card:** "Built to Last, Not Just to Look Good" — Warranty-backed materials, elevation-rated systems.
- **Team Statement:** "The cheapest option almost never lasts. We help you invest wisely."
- **CTA Support:** "Choose a roof that performs for decades — not one that just passes code."
- **Visual:** Side-by-side of new install and 10-year-old Highlander roof still performing

### 5. Regional Pride
- **Definition:** Born in Western North Carolina. Built for mountain weather, mountain architecture, and mountain communities.
- **Trust Card:** "Built for These Mountains" — Snow loads, wind exposure, UV intensity — we spec for real conditions.
- **Team Statement:** "We live here. We build here. We're not passing through."
- **CTA Support:** "Work with a team that knows what 4,000 feet of elevation does to a roof."
- **Visual:** Drone shot with mountain backdrop, WNC landscape, team in local community

### 6. Professionalism
- **Definition:** Clean job sites. On-time arrivals. Branded crews. Respectful of your property and your neighbors.
- **Trust Card:** "Your Home Deserves Better Than a Messy Job Site" — Daily cleanup, tarped landscaping, magnetic nail sweep.
- **Team Statement:** "We treat your property the way we'd treat our own — maybe better."
- **CTA Support:** "Experience what it's like to work with a contractor who actually respects your home."
- **Visual:** Clean jobsite photo, tarped landscaping, organized material staging

### 7. Project Care
- **Definition:** Every project gets the same attention whether it's a $5,000 repair or a $50,000 installation.
- **Trust Card:** "Every Project. Same Standard." — Owner oversight on every job. No crew gets unsupervised.
- **Team Statement:** "We don't have a B-team. Every crew operates to the same standard."
- **CTA Support:** "Your project gets the same crew quality and attention as our largest installations."
- **Visual:** Owner on-site inspecting work, crew working methodically on smaller repair

## Reusable Section Formats

### ValuesPillarGrid
- 7 cards, each with icon, value name, one-liner, and "overcomes" objection
- Use on: Homepage (compact 4), About (full 7), Team (selected 3-4)

### ValuesEditorial
- Full-width alternating layout: large value statement left, supporting copy + visual right
- Use on: About page deep section

### ValuesTrustBar
- Single-line strip: icon + value name repeated horizontally
- Use on: Below forms, footer area, service page mid-sections

### ValuesTeamOverlay
- Team member card with their personal value statement
- Use on: Team page, about page leadership section

### ValuesCTASupport
- Small text block below CTA buttons reinforcing the relevant value
- Use on: Every CTA section — pick the value most relevant to the page context

## Page Mapping
