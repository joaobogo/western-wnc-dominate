## Market priority
- **Primary:** Franklin NC, Highlands NC, Cashiers NC
- **Secondary:** Sylva NC, Cullowhee NC

## Article-per-city rule
Each city-specific article targets ONE primary location. Do not try to rank one article for all five cities. Other cities may be mentioned only where natural (e.g. describing broader service area).

## Anti-duplication requirements for city variants
Each variant of a shared topic (e.g. "Roof Repair vs. Roof Replacement: [City]") must be **70–80%+ unique** and include its own:
- Unique intro
- Local homeowner concerns
- Local property/roof characteristics
- Different examples
- Different FAQs
- Different headings where appropriate
- Unique meta title + description
- Local internal links
- Local image alt text
- Local CTA wording

Never city-swap. Never list every service city in a paragraph. Never keyword-stuff city names.
