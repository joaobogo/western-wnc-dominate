# Content & Editorial Strategy

## Strategic Purpose
Transform Highlander's website from a brochure into a regional authority platform. Every article should serve at least 2 of: SEO, Trust, Conversion, Education, Local Relevance.

## Content Pillars (9 Categories)

### 1. Roofing Education
- **Purpose:** Establish expertise, capture informational SEO traffic
- **Topics:** How roofing works, material comparisons, terminology, what to expect
- **Tone:** Teacher — clear, patient, authoritative
- **SEO Target:** "how to [roofing topic] western nc"
- **Conversion Path:** Inline CTA → Free Inspection

### 2. Construction Insights
- **Purpose:** Prove construction division credibility, educate on additions/renovations
- **Topics:** Home additions, outdoor living, renovation planning, building codes, permits
- **Tone:** Advisor — experienced, practical
- **SEO Target:** "home addition [WNC town]", "deck builder western nc"
- **Conversion Path:** Inline CTA → Schedule Consultation

### 3. Storm & Weather Updates
- **Purpose:** Timely relevance, emergency trust, insurance guidance
- **Topics:** Storm damage response, seasonal weather prep, insurance claims process
- **Tone:** Urgent but calm — "here's what to do"
- **SEO Target:** "storm damage roof [town] nc", "ice dam prevention mountain homes"
- **Conversion Path:** Emergency CTA → Call (828) 524-7773

### 4. Homeowner Guidance
- **Purpose:** Build trust through helpful, non-salesy advice
- **Topics:** Choosing a contractor, avoiding scams, when to repair vs replace, financing
- **Tone:** Honest neighbor — no agenda, just good advice
- **SEO Target:** "how to choose roofing contractor nc"
- **Conversion Path:** Soft CTA → "Questions? We're happy to help."

### 5. Project Planning
- **Purpose:** Help prospects plan and budget, reduce friction to conversion
- **Topics:** Cost breakdowns, timelines, what to expect, project checklists
- **Tone:** Planner — organized, thorough, transparent
- **SEO Target:** "roof replacement cost [town] nc", "home addition timeline"
- **Conversion Path:** Estimator tool or Schedule Consultation

### 6. Material Guides
- **Purpose:** Deep expertise on roofing and construction materials
- **Topics:** Metal vs shingle, cedar performance, underlayment, siding, decking materials
- **Tone:** Specialist — detailed, comparative, evidence-based
- **SEO Target:** "best roofing material for [climate condition]"
- **Conversion Path:** Material selector tool or consultation CTA

### 7. Maintenance Content
- **Purpose:** Retain past clients, attract maintenance-stage searches
- **Topics:** Seasonal checklists, preventive maintenance, annual inspection guides
- **Tone:** Coach — encouraging, proactive
- **SEO Target:** "spring roof maintenance checklist", "winter roof preparation [town]"
- **Conversion Path:** Schedule maintenance inspection

### 8. Local WNC Content
- **Purpose:** Regional SEO dominance, community connection
- **Topics:** Town-specific guides, local building trends, community involvement, elevation-specific advice
- **Tone:** Local insider — "we live here too"
- **SEO Target:** "[town] nc roofing", "building in [county]"
- **Conversion Path:** Town-specific service page link

### 9. Company Proof Content
- **Purpose:** Build credibility through transparency
- **Topics:** Project spotlights, team introductions, process behind-the-scenes, certification explanations
- **Tone:** Proud but humble — "here's what we did and how"
- **SEO Target:** Brand searches, "highlander roofing reviews"
- **Conversion Path:** Project detail pages or consultation CTA

## Editorial Tone Rules
1. Never write generic filler — every article must have a WNC-specific angle
2. Always include elevation, county, or town references where relevant
3. Use real numbers (costs, timelines, measurements) — not vague ranges
4. End every article with a specific, relevant CTA — not "call us"
5. Include internal links to service pages, town pages, and related articles
6. Reference Highlander credentials naturally — never force them

## Content-to-Conversion Mapping
- **Top of funnel** (education, materials, maintenance) → Soft CTAs, lead magnets, newsletter
- **Middle of funnel** (planning, tips, local) → Service page links, estimator tools
- **Bottom of funnel** (storm, proof, project spotlights) → Direct consultation CTAs
- **Retention** (maintenance, seasonal) → Annual inspection scheduling

## Editorial Calendar Framework (Quarterly)
- **Q1 (Jan-Mar):** Winter prep follow-up, ice dam content, spring planning guides
- **Q2 (Apr-Jun):** Spring maintenance, project planning season, material guides
- **Q3 (Jul-Sep):** Storm season content, outdoor living features, construction spotlights
- **Q4 (Oct-Dec):** Fall maintenance, winter prep, year-in-review, community content

## SEO Article Structure
1. H1: Primary keyword + local modifier
2. Opening paragraph with keyword and local context
3. H2 sections answering specific questions (featured snippet targets)
4. Internal links to 2-3 relevant service/town pages
5. Author attribution (team member name for E-E-A-T)
6. Schema: Article + LocalBusiness
7. Meta title < 60 chars, meta description < 160 chars
