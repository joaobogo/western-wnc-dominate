# Global CTA Language Architecture

## Primary CTA Labels (gold gradient buttons)
- **Homepage/Nav/Sticky:** "Start Your Project"
- **Roofing pages:** "Request a Roof Consultation"
- **Construction pages:** "Schedule a Project Consultation"
- **About/Reviews/Certs/Blog:** "Talk With Our Team"
- **Commercial B2B:** "Discuss Your Building"
- **Footer strip:** "Discuss Your Project"
- **Mid-page roofing:** "Talk With a Roofing Advisor"
- **Blog inline:** "Request a Consultation"
- **Storm context:** "Request an Assessment"

## Secondary CTA
- Phone: "Call Direct" — never "Call Now"

## Forbidden Language
Never use: "Free inspection/estimate/quote", "Get started", "Get a free...", "Claim your...", "Book now", "Buy now", "Act now", "Limited time", "Click here", "Don't miss out", "Hurry"

All constants defined in `src/lib/cta-config.ts`
