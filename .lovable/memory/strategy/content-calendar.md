# 6-Month Content Calendar

## Publishing Cadence
- **8 articles/month** (2 per week)
- Mix: 3 evergreen, 2 seasonal/timely, 2 local/WNC, 1 proof/company
- Total over 6 months: **48 articles**

## Content Mix Rules
1. Never publish 2 articles in the same category back-to-back
2. Every month must include at least 1 local WNC article
3. Storm content publishes within 48 hours of major weather events (bonus, not counted)
4. Every article must link to at least 1 service page + 1 town page
5. Alternate funnel stages: never 3 TOFU articles in a row without a MOFU/BOFU piece
