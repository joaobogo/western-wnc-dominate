# Unified Content Engine Blueprint

## Mission Statement
Transform Highlander's website from a contractor brochure into Western North Carolina's premier roofing and construction authority platform — one that educates, builds trust, improves rankings, and generates high-quality leads through content that feels written by real experts who live and work in the mountains.

---

## 1. Blog Hub Strategy

**Reference:** `mem://strategy/content-strategy`

### Hub Page (`/blog`)
- Premium editorial design — NOT a basic contractor blog
- Hero with featured/pinned article (full-width, magazine feel)
- Category filter chips with color-coded accents
- Trending/timely section (storm content, seasonal)
- WNC News section (local relevance)
- Search + filter by category, town, date
- Newsletter signup with value prop ("Mountain homeowner insights, not spam")
- 3-column grid on desktop, single column on mobile
- Infinite scroll or "Load more" (not pagination)

### Publishing Cadence
- 8 articles/month (2/week)
- Mix: 3 evergreen, 2 seasonal/timely, 2 local, 1 proof
- Storm bonus articles within 48 hours of major events

## 3. Article Template System

### Standard Article Structure
1. **Hero:** Category badge + H1 with primary keyword + local modifier
2. **Byline:** Author photo, name, role, certification, date, read time
3. **Editorial Trust Strip:** "Expert Written · Locally Researched · Fact-Checked"
4. **Key Takeaways:** 3-5 bullet summary box (featured snippet target)
5. **Article Body:** H2 sections answering specific questions
   - Expert Commentary callouts (max 2)
   - Local Expertise Notes (max 3)
   - Inline CTAs at natural decision points
   - Internal links (1 per 100 words max)
6. **Trust Sidebar (desktop):** Related services, certifications, review count
7. **FAQ Block:** 3-5 questions with FAQPage schema
8. **Author Bio Card:** Full bio with link to author archive
9. **Related Articles:** 3-card grid
10. **Closing CTA:** Category-appropriate conversion block

### Template Variants
- **Educational:** Diagrams, comparison tables, numbered lists. Teacher tone.
- **Storm/Urgent:** Alert-style header, amber accents, phone CTA prominent. Calm but urgent.
- **Local/WNC:** Warm, community-focused. Town/county references, local landmarks.
- **Project Spotlight:** Photo-heavy, magazine layout. Before/after showcases.
- **Planning:** Checklist-heavy, budget frameworks, timelines. Organized tone.

- Vary anchor text for same target

## 11. Author & Expertise System

**Reference:** `mem://strategy/expertise-system`

### Modules (7)
1. **Author Byline** — photo, name, role, cert, date, read time (every article)
2. **Reviewed By Badge** — for technical/claims/code articles
3. **Expert Commentary Callout** — inline quote card (max 2/article)
4. **Local Expertise Note** — WNC-specific data callout (max 3/article)
5. **Author Bio Card** — full bio at article footer
6. **Editorial Trust Strip** — "Expert Written · Locally Researched · Fact-Checked"
7. **Project Expert Commentary** — crew notes on project detail pages

### Anti-Patterns
- Never publish without named author
- Never use stock headshots
- Never use "Admin" or "Team" as author
- Never let quotes sound corporate
- Never overload with callouts

### Schema
- Article + Person (author) + reviewedBy on every article
- LocalBusiness on every page
- FAQPage on FAQ sections
