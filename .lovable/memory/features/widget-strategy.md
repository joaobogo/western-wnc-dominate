# Widget Strategy

## Design Principles
- Every widget must provide **real value** before asking for anything
- Visual style: clean cards with brand colors, generous padding, subtle animations
- Mobile: full-width, touch-friendly, no horizontal scrolling
- No email gates before showing results
- Each widget has a natural conversion endpoint

---

## Widget 1: Roofing Project Type Selector

**Purpose:** Help visitors self-identify their roofing need and route to the right page
**Placement:** Roofing Division page (hero area), Homepage (ServicesGrid alternative)
**UI:** Visual card grid — 6 cards with icons, titles, and 1-line descriptions
**Cards:** Roof Replacement, Roof Repair, Storm Damage, Commercial, Specialty Materials, Inspection
**Mobile:** 2-column grid, tappable cards
**Style:** Cream cards on green background, subtle hover elevation, brand icons
**Trust value:** Shows breadth of expertise without overwhelming
**SEO value:** Internal links from high-traffic page to service pages
**Conversion:** Each card links to its service page → service page has its own CTA

## Widget 2: Repair vs. Replace Guidance Widget

**Purpose:** Help homeowners decide if they need a repair or full replacement
**Placement:** Roof Repair page, Roof Replacement page, /resources/repair-vs-replace, blog articles
**UI:** 5-question interactive quiz with visual answer cards
**Questions:**
1. How old is your roof? (<10 yrs / 10-20 / 20-30 / 30+)
2. How many layers of roofing? (1 / 2 / Not sure)
3. What's the issue? (Leak / Missing shingles / Storm damage / General aging / Multiple problems)
4. How much of the roof is affected? (Small area / One side / Multiple areas / Entire roof)
5. Have you had repairs before? (No / Once / Multiple times)

**Output:** "Based on your answers, [repair/replacement/professional assessment] is likely the best path."
**Mobile:** Full-width step cards, swipe or tap to advance
**Style:** Progress dots, green/amber/red indicator for result severity
**Trust value:** Honest — sometimes recommends repair even though replacement is more revenue
**SEO value:** Featured snippet target for "should I repair or replace my roof"
**Conversion:** "Want a professional opinion? Schedule a free inspection." → Roofing consultation form

## Widget 3: Roofing Materials Comparison Tool

**Purpose:** Compare roofing materials side-by-side with WNC-specific performance data
**Placement:** Material guide pages, Residential Roofing, Specialty Roofing, blog articles
**UI:** Interactive comparison table — select 2-3 materials to compare
**Materials:** Architectural Shingles, Standing Seam Metal, Cedar Shake, Synthetic, Copper/Specialty
**Comparison Axes:** Lifespan, Cost Range, Wind Rating, Snow Performance, UV Resistance, Maintenance, Warranty, Mountain Suitability Score
**Mobile:** Swipeable cards instead of table, one material per card with comparison highlights
**Style:** Clean data table with brand green headers, mountain suitability shown as star rating
**Trust value:** Data-driven, transparent, manufacturer-backed specs
**SEO value:** High — targets "metal vs shingle roofing" and material comparison queries
**Conversion:** "Not sure which is right for your home? Our team can help." → Material consultation

## Widget 4: Storm Response Checklist Widget

**Purpose:** Guide homeowners through immediate post-storm steps
**Placement:** Storm Center, Storm Damage page, post-storm alert banner
**UI:** Interactive checklist with expandable steps
**Steps:**
1. ☐ Ensure everyone is safe (expandable safety tips)
2. ☐ Document visible damage with photos (tips on what to photograph)
3. ☐ Cover any openings temporarily (safe methods only)
4. ☐ Contact your insurance company (what to say, what to ask)
5. ☐ Schedule a professional inspection (don't go on the roof yourself)
6. ☐ Save all documentation (receipts, photos, claim numbers)

**Mobile:** Full-width accordion, tappable checkboxes, progress counter
**Style:** Amber accent for urgency, checkmark animations, calm tone
**Trust value:** Extremely high — helpful without being salesy during a stressful moment
**SEO value:** Featured snippet for "what to do after storm damage roof"
**Conversion:** Step 5 links to storm assessment form. Final CTA: "Need help? Call (828) 397-9211"

## Widget 5: Project Consultation Planner

**Purpose:** Help users think through their project before requesting a consultation
**Placement:** Construction Division, /plan-your-project, custom construction pages
**UI:** Multi-step guided planner (not a form — a thinking tool)
**Steps:**
1. What do you want to achieve? (More space / Better kitchen / Outdoor living / Custom home)
2. What matters most? (Budget / Timeline / Design / Resale value)
3. Where are you in planning? (Dreaming / Researching / Ready to plan / Have plans)
4. What's your property like? (Flat lot / Slope / Mountain top / In town)

**Output:** Personalized "Your Project Profile" summary with recommended next steps and relevant resources
**Mobile:** Full-screen steps with large tappable options
**Style:** Warm construction palette, aspirational photography per step
**Trust value:** Positions Highlander as a thoughtful planning partner
**SEO value:** Low (interactive, not indexable) — but drives engagement metrics
**Conversion:** "Ready to bring this to life? Schedule a project consultation."

## Widget 7: Construction Project Fit Guide

**Purpose:** Help users understand if their project idea is feasible and what's involved
**Placement:** Construction pages, /resources/planning guides
**UI:** 4-question interactive guide
**Questions:**
1. What type of project? (Addition / Renovation / Outdoor / Custom)
2. Do you have HOA or covenant restrictions? (Yes / No / Not sure)
3. What's your lot situation? (Flat / Moderate slope / Steep / Ridgeline)
4. Do you need permits? (Yes / Probably / Not sure)

**Output:** "Your Project Fit Summary" — what to expect, common challenges for your situation, and recommended planning steps
**Mobile:** Full-width step cards
**Style:** Clean informational cards with green/amber/red indicators for complexity
**Trust value:** Honest about challenges — builds trust through transparency
**SEO value:** Low-medium — targets "building on steep lot WNC"
**Conversion:** "Want expert guidance? Our team builds on mountain lots every day."

## Widget 8: Service Area Finder

**Purpose:** Help users confirm Highlander serves their area and connect with local context
**Placement:** Service Areas page, footer (all pages), location step in forms
**UI:** Interactive map of WNC with clickable town markers OR searchable town dropdown
**Interaction:** Click/select a town → shows: services available, project count, local team, distance/drive time
**Mobile:** Dropdown selector (map too complex on small screens), results card below
**Style:** Topographic-style map with green markers, cream info cards
**Trust value:** Local presence validation — "we're not a distant company"
**SEO value:** High — each town card links to town page, strengthening local SEO
**Conversion:** "We serve [Town]. Schedule a local consultation." → Town-specific form

---

## Widget 9: Consultation Scheduling Helper

**Purpose:** Reduce friction between "I'm interested" and "I have an appointment"
**Placement:** Confirmation pages, chatbot handoff, service page CTAs
**UI:** Day/time selector (not a full calendar — simplified availability grid)
**Shows:** Next 5 available days, morning/afternoon slots
**Interaction:** Select day → select time → confirm contact info → booked
**Mobile:** Vertical day list with tappable time slots
**Style:** Clean calendar UI, green for available, muted for unavailable
**Trust value:** Shows professionalism and availability — "they're organized"
**SEO value:** None (interactive only)
**Conversion:** Direct booking — highest conversion intent widget
