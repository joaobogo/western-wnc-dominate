# Construction Planning & Project Fit Tools

## Design Philosophy
These tools help homeowners **think clearly** about their construction project before they ever talk to a contractor. They position Highlander as a thoughtful planning partner, not a "get your instant quote" factory. Every tool ends with a natural invitation to continue the conversation with a real person.

---

## Tool 1: Project Readiness Quiz

**Purpose:** Help users assess whether they're ready to start a construction project
**Placement:** Construction Division page, /plan-your-project, construction blog articles

### Questions (7)
1. **What are you hoping to build?**
   Addition / Renovation / Outdoor living / Custom home / Not sure yet

2. **Do you own the property?**
   Yes / Under contract / Renting (→ gentle exit: "Talk with your landlord first")

3. **Have you checked local building codes or HOA restrictions?**
   Yes / No / Not sure / No HOA

4. **Do you have plans, sketches, or inspiration?**
   Detailed plans / Rough sketches / Pinterest board / Nothing yet

5. **What's your budget situation?**
   Have a firm budget / Have a general range / Need to figure it out / Planning to finance

6. **What's your timeline?**
   Want to start ASAP / Next 3-6 months / This year / Just exploring

7. **Have you worked with a contractor before?**
   Yes, good experience / Yes, mixed experience / No, first time

## Tool 5: Renovation Goals Builder

**Purpose:** Help homeowners clarify and prioritize their renovation goals

**Step 1: What rooms are you renovating?** (multi-select)

**Step 2: For each room, what's the goal?** (select per room)
- Cosmetic refresh (paint, fixtures, surfaces)
- Functional upgrade (layout, storage, appliances)
- Full renovation (gut and rebuild)
- Accessibility improvement

**Step 3: What's driving this?**
- We want to enjoy our home more
- Preparing to sell
- Just moved in — making it ours
- Damage or aging — it needs it
- Accessibility needs

**Step 4: What matters most?** (rank drag-and-drop or numbered)
- Staying on budget
- Getting it done quickly
- Design quality
- Minimal disruption to daily life
- Resale value impact

## Tool 6: Custom Project Consultation Prep Tool

**Purpose:** Help serious prospects organize their thoughts before a consultation
**Placement:** /plan-your-project, Custom Construction page, pre-consultation email link

### Guided Prep Questions

**Section 1: Your Vision**
- Describe your project in a few sentences (textarea)
- Upload inspiration photos (drag and drop, max 10)
- Style preference: Modern / Traditional / Rustic / Mountain Modern / Craftsman / Other

**Section 2: Practical Details**
- Property status: Own / Under contract / Looking
- Lot characteristics: Slope / Flat / Access / Utilities
- Do you have plans? Upload them here (PDF, images)
- Are you working with an architect or designer? Yes / No / Looking for one

**Section 3: Budget & Timeline**
- Budget range (optional — "This helps us tailor our recommendations")
- Ideal start date
- Must-complete-by date (if any)
- Financing: Cash / Loan / HELOC / Need guidance

**Section 4: Questions for Us**
- What questions do you have for our team? (textarea)
- What concerns you most about this project? (textarea)

Doesn't generate a "result" — instead packages everything into a structured consultation request that the Highlander team receives before the meeting.

## Shared Design & UX Rules

1. **No instant pricing promises** — always "ranges" and "planning-level estimates"
2. **WNC context** — every tool mentions elevation, weather, or local factors where relevant
3. **Results without gates** — show everything, then offer to email
4. **Mobile-first** — all tools are step cards on mobile, never side-by-side layouts
5. **Progress indicators** — dots or bars showing step count
6. **Back button** — always available, never lose progress
7. **Save/resume** — sessionStorage persistence for multi-step tools
8. **Analytics:** Track starts, completions, drop-off steps, CTA clicks per tool
9. **Disclaimer:** "Planning-level guidance. Free professional consultation recommended for accuracy."
