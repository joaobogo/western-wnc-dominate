# Off-site SEO checklist (October 2026)

The website side is fixed: fast pages, clean titles and descriptions, and page
titles aimed at the searches stuck on page 2. Most of what still holds the local
rankings back happens off the website. Work through this list in order.

## 1. Google Business Profile (biggest lever)
Most of the "position 1, zero clicks" searches in Search Console are map-pack
impressions from the Business Profile, so this is where local rankings move most.

- [ ] **Fix the website link.** Change it from `https://www.highlandernc.com/?utm_...`
      to `https://highlandernc.com/` (no www). Use the UTM version only if GA4 needs it,
      and keep it on the non-www domain.
- [ ] **Reviews.** Ask every finished job for a Google review, and ask them to mention
      the town and the work ("metal roof in Lake Glenville"). Aim for 4 to 6 a month.
      Reply to every review within 48 hours.
- [ ] **Photos.** Upload 5 to 10 real job photos a month, using the same jobs as the
      website (Franklin, Sylva, Cullowhee, Cashiers, Lake Glenville).
- [ ] **Posts.** One post a week: a finished job, a seasonal tip, or an offer. Link
      each post to the matching service page.
- [ ] **Categories.** Primary: Roofing contractor. Secondary: General contractor,
      Deck builder, Gutter service, Construction company.
- [ ] **Service areas.** List the towns from the website's service area page.
- [ ] **Second profile.** Check the Sylva showroom has its own verified profile,
      with the same name, phone format and hours as the website.
