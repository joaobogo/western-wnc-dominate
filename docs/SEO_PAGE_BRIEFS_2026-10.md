# SEO briefs for the 20 priority pages (8 Oct 2026)

Prepared for the NeuronWriter pass. **No page copy was changed for this document.** Measurements come from the built, prerendered site (368 pages); SERP notes come from live searches on 8 Oct 2026. Suggested titles, H1s and descriptions are proposals only. Constraints for every rewrite: title 60 characters or fewer ending `| Highlander`; description 110 to 155 characters; no em dashes; "free estimate" (never "free inspection"); rating, address, phone and hours only from `src/data/business.ts`; no unconfirmed claims (CertainTeed tier, coverage types, response times, subcontracting).

## What the SERPs look like

- **Local "roofers in [town]" terms** (Franklin, Highlands, Cashiers, Sylva) are dominated by HomeAdvisor, Angi, GAF contractor listings, Porch and Houzz. Own-site pages win on specifics those directories cannot match: elevation, rain, permitting, showroom, named projects.
- **"Metal roofing western NC"** is owned by one competitor with a whole content hub (laneroofingasheville.com: guide, lifespan, benefits, cost, climate-specific installation). To compete, `/roofing/metal` needs supporting depth, not just a longer page.
- **Metal roof cost** results are cost-per-square-foot ranges ($4 to $22 per sq ft by material). The page that answers with a clear table wins the click. Our cost page does this, but the pricing stance is still an open owner decision.
- **"Home additions western NC"** is Houzz, BuildZoom, HomeAdvisor and Asheville builders. Almost nobody local to Macon/Jackson County ranks, so this is the most winnable construction term.
- **Off-site problem (fix before ranking work pays off):** HomeAdvisor and similar listings still show "Highlander Roofing Services, Inc.", **1511 Highlands Rd**, rated 4.52 from 24 reviews. The site says Highlander Building Services, 40 Depot Street, 4.8. Mismatched name, address and rating across citations weakens local rankings. Update HomeAdvisor, Angi, Porch, Houzz, GAF, BBB and Google Business Profile.

## Findings that apply to all 20 pages

1. **17 of the 20 pages have no H2 containing the target phrase** (only the three Franklin and Highlands service-town pages do, 2 each). Add keyword variants to roughly 3 H2s per page.
2. **Sentence-length H2s.** Several pages use a whole paragraph as an H2 (repair, replacement, storm damage, additions, outdoor living, renovations, construction). Turn them into paragraphs and write short question or keyword H2s.
3. **Boilerplate H2s on every page** ("Start with three simple contact details.", "How should Highlander reach you?", "Who shows up at your property.", "Pick the next step that fits"). They add nothing to relevance and dilute the outline. Make them non-heading text.
4. **Em dashes** remain in many H2s and body copy (titles and descriptions are now stripped automatically). Rewrite them in the NeuronWriter pass.
5. **Town pages waste title space.** Titles are 33 to 37 characters of a 60 budget.
6. **Internal links:** the cost pages are under-linked (`/roofing/metal/cost` is linked from only 7 pages, `/construction/renovations` from 12, `/roofing-cost-western-nc` from 25; everything else gets about 360 mostly from the sitewide menu and footer).
7. **Schema:** every page has Service, LocalBusiness and Breadcrumb. Keep own-business review markup off.
8. **Fixed today:** six titles with em dashes, and the `/roofing` hero photo was labeled Cashiers although the project data says Highlands.
